    About the maker

    A personal offering to Bharat

    I am a Sanātani who believes Bharat carries a complete and abundant civilizational inheritance of its own. I made Hidden India to honour and share our temples, tīrthas, forts, sacred traditions and historical knowledge with care. I hope people across the nation will help preserve the stories and wisdom our ancestors have left for us.

    Help grow the collection: You can suggest a place, share a photograph you have the right to share, and provide information or sources. This version is hosted as a free static website, so it cannot yet receive and review submissions centrally, save them to a shared database, or issue cash rewards. Those features need a secure server, moderation and a lawful payment arrangement.
